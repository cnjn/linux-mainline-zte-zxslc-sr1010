Patch 0013: net: ethernet: zte: add ZX279133 LAN conduit

Scope:
- built-in zx279133-lan-conduit platform child
- typed zx279133_lan_service boundary
- shared WAN/LAN NPPT, IDM, RX/NAPI, TX-ring and runtime-PM ownership
- LAN source-port 5/59..62 demultiplexing and private transport VLAN 59..62
- no RTL8372N switch or DSA tagger implementation in this patch

Changes:
- add independently owned lan-cpu0 instead of allocating the conduit netdev in
  the NPPT probe
- populate LAN child devices only after eth0 and the typed parent service are
  ready
- add first-user/last-user shared hardware ownership and fail-closed TX quiesce
- retain the physical eth0 queue as shared-ring BQL owner while preserving
  per-origin packet, byte, drop and completion accounting
- route LAN-originated TX to NP logical destination port 5
- demultiplex descriptor source 5 for LAN-only startup and 59..62 for concurrent
  WAN/LAN operation, preserving the corresponding private VLAN sideband for the
  later DSA tagger
