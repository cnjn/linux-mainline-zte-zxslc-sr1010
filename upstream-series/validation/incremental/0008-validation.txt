Patch 0008: net: phy: add ZTE ZX279051 support

Scope:
- MAINTAINERS
- drivers/net/phy/Kconfig
- drivers/net/phy/Makefile
- drivers/net/phy/zx279051.c
- drivers/net/phy/zx279051-fullmask.h

Changes:
- add a PHYLIB driver for the C22-enumerated ZX279051 using direct Clause 45
  transactions for its IEEE MMD and vendor register spaces
- replay the ordered post-external-reset initialization image and explicitly
  wake the PHY after the vendor sequence ends in BMCR power-down
- advertise 10/100/1000 half/full, 2500 full, pause and asymmetric pause from
  phydev->advertising; explicit autonegotiation disable is rejected with
  -EOPNOTSUPP because no evidence-backed forced-line-mode contract is known
- report link-partner 10/100, 1000 and 2500 advertisements and resolve pause only
  after the hardware duplex state is decoded
- configure the internal Uni-side SerDes for SGMII or 2500BASE-X from the live
  GE speed code, with checked APB transactions, bounded lock polling and cached
  successful host-mode state
- declare SGMII and 2500BASE-X as possible PHY interfaces and expose generic
  suspend/resume hooks
