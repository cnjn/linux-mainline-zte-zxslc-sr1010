### Negative resource findings

Across the ZX279133/SR1010-specific source set, searches found **no regulator consumers, power-domain attachments, IOMMU properties/API, OPP tables, interconnect consumers, or explicit thermal cooling devices**. Thermal participation is limited to the PVT OF thermal sensor. No custom procfs/debugfs/ioctl was found. ## Architecture

SR1010 enables the SoC clock/reset providers first, then PL011/GPIO/SFC/PVT/watchdogs/USB/MDIO. NPPT owns the large packet-processing register windows and reserved DMA memory, exposes WAN and LAN netdev paths, consumes PON/Uni SerDes Generic PHY providers and two generic DesignWare XPCS instances. The LAN conduit becomes the DSA CPU conduit; the RTL8372N child uses MDIO0 for switch management and port 8 as the 10G CPU port. MDIO1 hosts the PHY-ID-bound ZX279051 WAN PHY. Syscon-only nodes provide reset routing, PON routing, and IDM/CCI programming.

Key risks:
- Audit drivers hard-code physical addresses and may race production drivers; they are not production DTS nodes and must remain audit-only.
- NPPT parses five IRQs but source evidence shows requests only for RX and local-test (`zx279133.c:6520-6530`); verify whether aggregate/IDM/buffer-release are intentionally polling/unused.
- RTL8372N is board/hardware-specific despite a DSA-facing interface; large magic init tables/register assumptions need vendor documentation validation.
- No production binding was found for `zte,zx279133-lan-conduit`; it is described only as an NPPT child. The port-status audit compatible also lacks a source schema.
- XPCS DTS declares 8 MiB regions while audit constants use 2 MiB; reconcile whether the larger aperture is intentional (`dtsi:490-512` versus audit `:14-16`).
